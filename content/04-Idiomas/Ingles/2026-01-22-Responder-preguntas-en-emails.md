# Responding to questions in an email

When responding to questions in an email, you can begin by referring to the questions asked and the answers you've provided.

| Thanks for sending me your questions. | I've provided answers to your questions below. |     
| :------------------------------------ | :--------------------------------------------: |


Next, answer the questions in order. Refer to each question specifically.

| **In answer to your first question**, HR deals with the bonus system | **The answer to your question about** holidays is that they increase by one day per year. |     
| :------------------------------------ | :--------------------------------------------: |



| **Addressing your last question**, pay is decided by your manager |  
| :------------------------------------ |
