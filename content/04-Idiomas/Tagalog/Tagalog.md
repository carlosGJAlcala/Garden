**Saludos:**

- Good Morning – **Magandang Umaga**
    
- Good Afternoon – **Magandang Hapon**
    
- Good Evening – **Magandang Gabi**
    

---

**Frases comunes:**

- How are you? – **Kumusta ka?**
    
- What are you doing? – **Ano ang ginagawa mo?**
    
- What are you looking for? – **Ano ang hinahanap mo?**
    

---

**Adjetivos:**

- Beautiful – **Maganda**
    
- Handsome – **Pogi**
    

---

**Expresiones afectivas y de amistad:**

- Can I be your friend? – **Pwede ba akong makipag-kaibigan sa’yo?** / **Pwede ba kitang maging kaibigan?**
    
- I like you – **Gusto kita**
    
- I love you – **Mahal kita**
